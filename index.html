Section — Selected Research & ML Projects

NeuroLens-RAG: Multimodal Representation Learning, Interpretability & LLM Evaluation 2026

Compared three representation-learning paradigms — supervised multi-task, supervised-contrastive, and self-supervised contrastive — across GRU/Transformer backbones on a 6-class motor-decoding task, holding the encoder interface fixed across 30 repeated subject-level splits.

  • Built a multi-method interpretability framework (Saliency, Integrated Gradients, exact Shapley, LIME, CAV/TCAV) across 1,440 model–concept evaluations
  • Built a fully local RAG + LLM pipeline (MiniLM retrieval, cross-encoder reranking, quantized Llama-3.2-3B, LoRA fine-tuning); lifted structured-output compliance from 0/8 to 8/8
  • Improved domain-specific retrieval top-1 accuracy from 43.9% to 61.0%; diagnosed an LLM faithfulness failure and replaced free-form judging with a deterministic evidence-based verdict layer
Bayesian Generative Modeling for Latent Network Structure 2024–2026

Reframed brain-network community detection from descriptive clustering into formal Bayesian hypothesis testing, evaluating whether competing organizational principles were actually supported by the data.

  • Established non-degree-corrected hierarchical organization as best-supported by comparing 7 and 5 stochastic block model variants against the field-standard modularity heuristic, in rodent and human networks respectively
  • Showed human functional networks reorganize under affective demands by reweighting shared latent modes, not by redrawing community boundaries — invisible to conventional single-partition analysis
  • Extended the open-source graph-tool library to compute hierarchical community-membership marginals unavailable in its standard implementation
Deep Sequence & Graph Models for Brain Decoding and Representation Learning 2020–2022

Built GRU-based sequence models to classify 15 naturalistic movie clips from brain activity at ~90% accuracy, then extended the framework to graph neural networks over structural connectivity.

  • Reached 96% classification accuracy with GNNs, explaining ~60% of variance against RNN and CNN baselines
  • Validated GRU predictions via gradient-based attribution and causal network lesioning — ablating high-attribution networks caused significantly larger performance drops than random controls
  • Applied transfer learning from the frozen GRU representation to predict fluid intelligence and verbal IQ (p < 0.005 for both)
Multiple Kernel Learning for Structural–Functional Brain Modeling 2015–2019

Reformulated dynamical models of brain structure–function relationships as Multiple Kernel Learning problems with LASSO-regularized optimization.

  • Achieved 71% correlation with held-out functional connectivity
  • Learned a low-dimensional dynamical manifold of time-varying connectivity, clustering it into latent brain states linked back to anatomical structure
  • Validated latent-state structure across repeated splits (0.86–0.98 correlation) and an independent 100-subject HCP cohort without retraining
