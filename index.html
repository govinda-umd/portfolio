2026 NeuroLens-RAG: Multimodal Representation Learning, Explainable AI & LLM Evaluation

Self-supervised, multimodal learning aligning human brain signals during motor actions with text captions (HCP motor task, 100 subjects, 6 classes), achieving a 0.9 F1 score.

  • Explainable AI (CAV/TCAV): achieved 98% directional sensitivity of predictions to concepts derived via input attributions, using one probing framework reused unchanged across all three representation models
  • Built a two-stage RAG pipeline (domain-adapted bi-encoder retrieval plus cross-encoder reranking), lifting top-1 recall of literature claims from 40% to 63%
  • Measured LLM (Llama-3.2-3B) sycophancy (10/12 false positives) and overcame it by engineering deterministic guardrails from structured signals
  • Audited an earlier architecture comparison for confounds before trusting it: found a 1.83× parameter mismatch and a missing causal-attention mask, fixed both, and confirmed the fix by measuring information leakage directly (1.32 to 0.0)
  • Added targeted LoRA fine-tuning to the local LLM, lifting structured-output compliance from 0/8 to 8/8, while showing the compliance gain alone didn't make its reasoning trustworthy enough to use unsupervised

2024–2026 Probabilistic Graph Clustering: Bayesian Model Selection & Uncertainty Quantification

Developed Bayesian generative models, fit via a custom discrete-space MCMC sampler, to discover latent cluster structure across high-dimensional graphs (mouse resting-state fMRI, 172 regions).

  • Found the best latent explanation using the minimum description length (MDL) principle, generalizable across a population of graphs
  • Uncovered multi-solution posterior landscapes via Bayesian mixture models: 4 statistically indistinguishable co-dominant configurations (Friedman χ² = 3.05, p = 0.55), invisible to standard heuristics
  • Condensed precision/recall-style partition-overlap metrics probabilistically into a single interpretable score
  • Gated every model comparison on convergence across 5 independent MCMC chains, and proved a widely used heuristic (modularity maximization) is mathematically a special case of one compared model, one that forces a confident answer exactly where the principled version correctly abstains
  • Traced the multiplicity down to specific regions with a per-cluster sub-clustering step, pinpointing primary motor cortex as the specific driver of one cluster's instability

2020–2022 Deep Sequence Models for Brain Decoding

Built GRU-based sequence models to classify 15 naturalistic movie clips from brain activity at 89% accuracy (chance 8%); feedforward and convolutional baselines topped out at 36 to 55%.

  • A temporal-order ablation (shuffling time while preserving signal statistics) dropped accuracy from 90% to 54%, confirming the model relies on true sequence dynamics, not just overall signal level
  • Compressed 300 input brain regions into a 10-dimensional latent code (96% smaller) while retaining 88% accuracy, close to the full-size model's 89%; a linear (PCA) compression at the same size performed far worse
  • Cross-validated gradient-based input attributions against direct causal ablations, which dropped prediction accuracy by >35%
  • Froze the learned representation to predict two auxiliary behavioral variables from brain activity alone (p < 0.005 for both), matching a widely used published benchmark
  • Reused the exact same architecture, unchanged, on a second dataset with a different task and signal modality: it still classified well above chance, showing this was a reusable method rather than a model tuned to one dataset

2016–2018 Graph Signal Processing & Multiple Kernel Learning

Reformulated an ordinary differential equation as a weighted combination of spectral graph filters (fit via LASSO), mapping one relational view of a graph to another: 71% correlation, against 52% and 37% for two published baselines.

  • Embedded windowed functional-connectivity snapshots onto a low-dimensional manifold via spectral embedding, clustered them into 12 discrete brain states with a BIC-selected Gaussian mixture model, and sequenced them with a first-order Markov chain to synthesize latent brain-state time series at 87.7% state-assignment accuracy
  • Validated the Markov chain's reproducibility by comparing transition matrices and steady-state distributions across 11 independent train/test splits (mean correlation 0.93)
  • Validated with 250+ perturbation stress tests, repeated k-fold cross-validation, and zero-shot transfer to an independent, larger dataset, reaching 69% correlation
